========================================================
IMPORTANT JWT RULE
========================================================

IN SWAGGER:

PASTE ONLY JWT

CORRECT:

eyJhbGciOiJIUzM4NCJ9...........


WRONG:

Bearer eyJhbGciOiJIUzM4NCJ9...........


SWAGGER AUTOMATICALLY ADDS "Bearer".


IN CMD CURL:

USE:

-H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_JWT"
